# Evidence-Led Scientific Research

Research to resolve the requested uncertainty, not to accumulate links or produce
an impressive dossier. The optional `thinking-like-a-scientist` skill offers shared
reasoning and delivery principles when installed; this guide also works independently.

## Frame the information need

- Identify the requested decision/measurement, existing implementation, strongest
  plausible alternative and evidence that would change the decision.
- Inspect already retained applicable evidence first. Choose breadth and depth
  from uncertainty and consequences, not a quota of searches or source classes.
- Distinguish research-only scope from authorized implementation. Research does
  not authorize new compute, uploads, lifecycle changes or publication actions.

## Select applicable evidence

| Source class | What to inspect |
|---|---|
| Primary literature | Appropriate disciplinary registry, actual publication/version, definitions, errata and superseding work |
| Official documentation | Actual instrument/data format, software version, interfaces and applicability |
| Source code | The relevant implementation and callers, tests, issues and changes; not only its README |
| Datasets and benchmarks | Population, selection, uncertainty, independence, exclusions and tested domain |
| Community discussions | Leads and failure reports, cross-checked against primary documentation or reproducible behavior |

Choose the registry appropriate to the field; no particular literature service,
project command or credential is a universal prerequisite. Multiple mirrors of one
paper are not independent physical evidence. Citation counts and recency guide
discovery, not applicability or correctness.

## Verify and challenge

1. Retain legally accessible source documents locally and inspect the relevant
   passages before using them as support. A search snippet or download receipt is
   not evidence for unread content. Use
   the optional `citation-verifier` skill for selected-source identity if installed.
2. Investigate counterevidence for consequential claims: alternative methods,
   failed domains, incompatible assumptions and results that contradict the
   proposed explanation. Do not manufacture a ritual counter-query for a trivial lookup.
3. For mathematics, prefer authentic source and official machine-readable data;
   inspect units, normalization, covariance, approximation and physical domain.
   The optional `literature-tex-miner` skill can help when installed.
4. Resolve conflicts by identity, version, assumptions and discriminating checks,
   not by selecting the more prestigious or convenient answer.
5. Distinguish reported author performance, an independently reproduced result
   and your own executed comparison. Do not invent numbers, APIs or benchmarks.

Protect confidential material and credentials. Do not upload internal notes,
unpublished data or protected answers to external services without authorization.
Do not spawn research agents or launch expensive evaluations without an explicit
request or an applicable approved budget.

## Finish the requested work

Deliver the supported answer, inspected evidence, consequential uncertainty and
specific unresolved dependency. A comprehensive report is appropriate when requested;
ordinary research needs no new report file, JSON ledger or mandatory template.

For an authorized repair, once evidence supports an intervention, implement and
assess it at the actual consumer instead of stopping at a blueprint. Preserve
failed interventions; do not search indefinitely or invent evidence to close a gap.
