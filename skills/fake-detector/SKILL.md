# Scientific Shortcut Audit

Review evidence, not the contributor's identity or an adversarial persona. A grep
match is a lead, never a verdict.

## Look for

- Missing measured data replaced by constants, synthetic observations, stale cache
  or a different method without an explicit applicable contract and disclosure.
- Exceptions or unexpected NaN/Inf converted into favorable values or zero density.
  Valid support rejections and documented mathematical infinities are different.
- Reported samples, uncertainties or probabilities clipped to look plausible;
  justified model support, display limits and documented numerical algorithms are
  not automatically such clipping.
- Empirical coefficients without inspected provenance, or product-specific settings
  guessed instead of read from actual inputs.
- Expected test answers generated by the same unverified implementation; mocks or
  lint presented as numerical accuracy, integration or scientific calibration.
- Failing cases, plausible modes, nuisance terms or scenarios removed to obtain a
  preferred outcome; tuned cases described as untouched evaluation.
- Hard implementation work mislabeled as missing observations, or a failure label
  presented as the completed repair.

Simulated events, pseudo-experiments and controlled synthetic fixtures are legitimate
when their construction and use are explicit. Negative weights or signed estimates
are not inherently fabricated; inspect their mathematical/physical contract and
uncertainty treatment. Do not demand positivity by analogy with probabilities.

## Procedure

1. Trace the suspected value from actual source through calculation and consumer.
2. Read surrounding code, callers and tests. Preserve valid constants, exact
   identities, identifiers and documented policy settings.
3. Identify the reproducible behavior, scientific consequence and evidence that
   distinguishes it from a legitimate operation.
4. For read-only work, report findings and the smallest discriminating check.
   For authorized repair, correct the owning calculation/consumer and measure its
   effect with focused coverage; another guard or disclaimer is not a missing method.
5. Limit only the conclusion affected by unknown inputs. Do not fabricate a
   replacement, infer universal invalidity or certify uninspected paths.

Search broad exception handlers, clipping, default-valued required fields, sample
slices and fixture generators with the project's existing tools. No hardcoded scan
script, project layout or automatic report file is required.
